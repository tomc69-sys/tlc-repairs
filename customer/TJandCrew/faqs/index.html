Frequently asked questions

What types of power systems do you install?

Covers solar, generator, battery backup, and fully integrated off-grid and grid-tied systems with turnkey delivery.

Do you offer complete start-to-finish installation?

Yes — all projects are handled in-house including design, fabrication, installation, and final system testing.

Can you build custom systems for remote or off-grid properties?

Yes — specialized in custom-built solutions for cabins, ranches, and remote Montana properties.

What brands or equipment do you work with?

Certified Platinum Kohler Dealer with expertise in high-quality solar and power system components.

Do you provide maintenance and repairs after installation?

Yes — ongoing service, system maintenance, and repairs are available for both new and existing systems.

Do you handle warranties and service claims?

All warranty support is handled directly, eliminating the need for customers to work with third parties.

Are you licensed and certified?

Licensed Electrical Contractor and General Contractor with advanced certifications in power systems, generators, and fabrication.

How long have you been in business?

Over 24 years serving Montana with reliable power and electrical solutions.

Do you offer solutions for power outages or backup power?

Yes — generator and battery backup systems designed to keep homes and businesses running during outages.

How do I get started with a project or quote?

Simple consultation process to assess needs, design a custom system, and provide a clear project plan and estimate.
