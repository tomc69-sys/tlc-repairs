Signs you need a generator repair

Being without modern necessities in the event of an emergency is scary. That's why home and business owners invest in generators. Over time, these units can malfunction, leaving you high and dry during desperate times. Become aware of these signs that indicate you need a generator repair:

  • Physical wear and tear
  • Frequent leaks
  • Diagnostic alerts
  • Stalled starting times
