Key reasons to choose a Kohler generator

  • Reliability and durability : Kohler uses commercial-grade engines in its generators, which are built to withstand heavy workloads and harsh conditions. They are also built to be long-lasting.
  • Power and performance : They can handle significant power needs, including large appliances like air conditioners, while providing clean, high-quality power suitable for sensitive electronics.
  • Automatic and fast startup : When a power outage occurs, a Kohler generator can start automatically and restore power to your home in about 10 seconds.
  • Long-term operation : Unlike some other generators, they are designed to run for extended periods, even for days or weeks, until utility power is restored.
  • Quality features : They include corrosion-proof enclosures to protect the generator from the elements and remote monitoring systems to track performance.
  • Backed by a strong warranty : Many Kohler generators come with a five-year, 2,000-hour warranty, providing peace of mind for the investment.
  • National support : A network of authorized dealers is available nationwide for expert installation and service.
