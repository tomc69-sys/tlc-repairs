About Remote Power Systems

Veteran-Owned Expertise Built on 24 Years of Power Solutions

When you need reliable off-grid or backup power, you deserve a team that understands how systems work in real-world conditions. Remote Power Systems is a locally veteran-owned, multi-generational Montana business with over 24 years of experience serving residential and remote power needs across the state.

As both a Licensed Electrical Contractor and Licensed General Contractor, we deliver fully compliant, start-to-finish solutions designed to keep your home or property running when it matters most.

Our approach is simple: honest service, long-term reliability, and systems built to perform. Every project reflects our commitment to doing things right the first time.

Discover how veteran-led expertise and Montana roots combine to deliver power systems you can count on.

Where Craftsmanship Meets Certified Power System Knowledge

Our certifications aren't just credentials—they're proof of the depth we bring to every installation. Our team includes a Master Power Systems Technician, Master Diesel Generator Mechanic, and Platinum Kohler Dealer status, ensuring you receive expert guidance from consultation through long-term support.

We handle everything in-house, from custom solar fabrication and senior-level welding to complete system design tailored to your property's unique demands.

Whether you're building off-grid in the mountains or adding backup power to your homestead, we design systems that fit your lifestyle and environment.

As a multi-generational Montana business, we understand the challenges of powering remote properties, harsh winters, and the peace of mind that comes from knowing your system was built to last.

Let's talk about what reliable power looks like for your property.

You won't be handed off to subcontractors or left waiting for callbacks. Our turnkey service model covers consultation, design, installation, maintenance, and warranty support—all managed by our experienced team. We stand behind every system we install because your satisfaction and safety depend on it.

  • Veteran-owned and operated with 24+ years of residential and remote power experience
  • Dual licensing as both Electrical and General Contractor for fully compliant installations
  • Master-level certifications in power systems and diesel generator mechanics
  • In-house solar fabrication and custom welding for systems built to your exact specifications
  • Platinum Kohler Dealer delivering factory-backed generator solutions and support

Remote Power Systems is ready to design and install a custom solar, generator, or complete power system that keeps your property running strong. Reach out today to schedule a consultation and experience the difference that veteran-led craftsmanship and Montana expertise make. We're here to power your independence with systems built to perform.
