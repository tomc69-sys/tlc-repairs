What a first site includes

This is website design for a small-town shop, not a six-month agency project. Most first sites need the same things:

  • A home page that says who you are and how to reach you
  • Hours, phone, and a text or email path that actually works on a phone
  • Pages for the services you sell
  • A look that matches the business, not a leftover template
  • Titles and a sitemap so Google can find it
  • Help pointing a domain, or help getting one if you do not have it yet

After it is live

Hours change. A new service shows up. A link breaks. We can keep the site current so it does not rot the way an old page usually does.

How website work goes

  1. You tell us the shop What you do, who calls you, whether you have a domain or only Facebook. That is enough to start.
  2. You get a scope A written list of pages and a price. Nothing is built until you say yes to that number.
  3. You see it before it is public We show you the site, fix the words that are off, then point the domain and leave you with a link you can send.

Before you call about a website

No. This is for a Salmon-area shop, trade, or outfitter that needs a site people can find: name, phone, hours, services.

We can use the photos and the story. The website still needs its own pages so Google and a customer on a phone are not stuck inside Facebook.

Will I know the price first?

Yes. Website work is scoped up front. You see the page list and the number before anything is built.
