Questions before you call

Website work, computer repair, or a laptop. If yours is not here, call or text (406) 369-0838.

Can you build a website?

Yes. Website design is for local shops that need a real site people can find. Live work includes Richie Outfitters. You get a scope and a price before work starts.

Do I have to bring the computer to a store?

No. We can meet you or drive to your location. I don’t mind the travel — a hundred miles from Salmon isn’t a problem. Call or text a time that works.

How far will you drive?

I don’t mind the travel, and a hundred miles from Salmon isn’t a problem. The first 20 miles are included with on-site service. Past that is $0.50 per mile, round trip. Call or text and I’ll estimate the travel with the job.

Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. Bench repair is $45/hour if you bring the computer in or we meet in town. On-site is $55/hour within 20 miles of Salmon, one-hour minimum. Travel past 20 miles is $0.50/mile round trip. I don’t mind the travel — a hundred miles from Salmon isn’t a problem. Call or text and I’ll estimate the service and travel. Website work is a written list of pages and a number. Parts are quoted before we order them.

Do you repair laptops and desktops?

Yes. Computer repair covers towers and shop PCs. Laptop repair covers screens, charging, heat, and tune-ups.

How fast can you look at it?

Call or text a time that works. Hours are Monday–Friday, 8am–7pm. Saturday is off. Sunday is by appointment. Most computer jobs are days, not weeks.

What if it is not worth fixing?

We will say so. Repair first is the point of this shop. A new computer is the last option, not the first. You decide after you hear the price.

Do you fix phones and tablets?

Not yet. Phone and tablet repair is coming soon. Call anyway if that is the job and we will tell you whether we can take it.

How do I get on the schedule?

Call (406) 369-0838 or text this number. Say what the machine is doing and where you are. Facebook messages work too if that is easier.

Still have a question?

Call or text. A real person answers. Tell us it is a computer, a laptop, or a website.
