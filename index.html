What we repair

If it plugs in, boots up, or sits on a desk, we can usually help. If we cannot fix it here, we will tell you that up front.

  • Computers & laptops

    Won’t start, overheating, cracked screens, slow Windows machines, Mac troubleshooting, and parts replacement.

  • Phones & tablets

    Charging problems, screen damage, software issues, and backup before a repair or trade-in.

  • Virus & malware

    Pop-ups, ransom notes, hijacked browsers, and “my computer got weird after an email.” Cleanup plus prevention.

  • Data backup & recovery

    Copy photos and files off a failing drive when possible. Set up simple backups so it does not happen twice.

  • Upgrades & tune-ups

    SSD and memory upgrades, Windows cleanup, and making an older PC usable again without buying new.

  • Electronics & peripherals

    Printers, Wi-Fi, TVs and monitors, power supplies, and other household electronics that stopped behaving.

How a repair works

  1. Call or text Tell us what is going on. We will meet you or drive to you.
